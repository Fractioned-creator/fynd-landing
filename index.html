What does "refurbished" mean at Fynd.club?

Every laptop is inspected, security-checked and tested component by component. We wipe all previous data, clean it thoroughly and fix or replace anything that doesn't meet our standard before it's listed.

How do grades A, B and C differ?

Grade A is like new, with hardly a mark. Grade B shows light signs of use. Grade C has visible cosmetic wear but works perfectly. All grades are functionally identical and carry the same warranty.

What does the warranty cover?

Every laptop comes with a 6-month warranty from the date of delivery, covering hardware faults that aren't caused by accidental damage or misuse.

Can I return a laptop?

Yes. You have 10 days from delivery to return it if it isn't right for you, as set out in our return policy.

Can I upgrade the RAM or SSD?

Yes. On supported models you can choose more memory or a larger SSD when you order. We fit and test the upgrade before shipping.

How does selling my laptop work?

Fill in the sell form above. Our team calls you with an offer; if you accept, we collect the laptop, run a security check and pay you. Your data is wiped securely.
